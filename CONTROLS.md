# Iron Circuit Controls

Welcome to the command line! Use these controls to pilot your chassis and test the prototype.

## Gameplay Controls
- **Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right):** Turn your robot toward the indicated direction. This determines where your weapons and directional attacks will be fired.
- **Keys 1, 2, 3, 4, 5:** Select a Program Card from your loadout to designate your movement or action this turn.
- **Enter / Return:** Confirm your selected facing and program card, and execute your turn.

## Prototype & Debug Controls
- **Escape Key:** Exit the game immediately.
- **N Key:** Skip the current level and jump directly to the next reward screen / sector loop.
- **F8 Key:** Replay a system debug audio probe (Laser sound) to test your audio output setup.

## Mechanics Overview
Each turn proceeds as follows:
1. You select a directional facing and a movement/attack card.
2. The turn is physically simulated: First, you step or dash along the card's defined path.
3. Next, your equipped weapon fires directly in your facing direction.
4. Then, surviving enemies execute their pre-telegraphed moves and attacks.
